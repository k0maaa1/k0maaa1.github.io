Channel Dashboard

Channel Dashboard is a private desktop tool used by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels. It runs locally on the owner's computer and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Shows statistics and analytics (views, watch time, subscribers) for the owner's channels.
  • Uploads and manages videos on the owner's channels.
  • Reads comments on the owner's videos.

The app accesses Google data only after the channel owner signs in and grants permission through Google's consent screen.

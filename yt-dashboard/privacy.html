Privacy Policy — Channel Dashboard

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Who uses this app

Channel Dashboard is a private tool used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not distributed to other users.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's OAuth consent screen, the app accesses:

  • YouTube channel and video information, statistics and analytics;
  • comments on the owner's videos;
  • the ability to upload, update and delete videos on the owner's channels;
  • the Google account's basic profile (name and email), used to show which account a channel belongs to.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to display statistics and manage videos for the owner.
  • OAuth tokens and fetched data are stored locally on the owner's computer, with tokens encrypted by the operating system.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further data access; locally stored data can be deleted by removing the app's data folder.
